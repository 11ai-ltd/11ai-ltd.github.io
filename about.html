About 11AI

Making intelligent machines safe to trust.

11AI is a UK technology company building the platforms that robotics teams need to develop, secure and validate their systems.

Our mission

Robots the world can rely on

Robots are leaving controlled settings and moving into factories, infrastructure and public spaces. As they become more autonomous and more connected, trust becomes essential.

We believe security and assurance should be part of how robots are developed from the start, not added at the end. Our work gives organisations the confidence to deploy intelligent machines responsibly.

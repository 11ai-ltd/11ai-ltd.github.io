Built for real environments

From the lab to the field

OmniCore is designed around physical robots working in complex, changing spaces. It keeps the full picture in one place, from how a robot perceives the world to how well it holds up under pressure.

  • Understanding of the environment and the mission
  • Ongoing visibility of risk as systems change
  • Findings that are clear, ranked and ready to act on
